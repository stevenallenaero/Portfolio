01 / AERODYNAMICSXFLR5 · MATLAB

Aircraft Design &
Performance Study

Developed an aircraft configuration with low drag, useful aerodynamic performance, and longitudinal stability. Exported XFLR5 aerodynamic results to MATLAB for aircraft-level range, endurance, and maneuver calculations.

  • Final analysis at 10 m/s: approximately 33.52 km range and 0.93 hr endurance.
  • Peak lift-to-drag ratio around 12–13; minimum drag near 0.045.
  • About 28 m turn radius and 20.5°/s turn rate at 20° bank.
  • Pitching-moment slope approximately −0.057/deg for static stability evaluation.

Modeled aircraft pitch dynamics to study how feedback changes stability, transient response, and steady-state behavior.
